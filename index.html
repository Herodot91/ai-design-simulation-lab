AI Design Simulation Lab

Transit ticket app · from raw code to a real-world 3D demo.
The Plumbing. A raw map of servers, passenger routes and API endpoints. Without mapping where ticket data is stored, we can't create the digital ticket.
Like drawing plumbing lines before deciding where the bathroom goes.
The Blueprint. Low-fidelity grayscale layouts focused on clicking, navigating and logic. No logos, no colours.
Where is the map? Where is the ticket? Find the simplest way to travel.
The Interior Design. Pixel-perfect style: brand colours, user cards and scan-ready QR codes. This is the high-fidelity skin that makes the app feel user-ready.
Click any screen or its buttons (Get ticket, tabs, route cards) to try the flow. The highlighted screen is the one shown in the 3D simulation.
The Cinematic Reveal. High-fidelity mockup + text prompt = a simulated real-world device presentation. Compare how lighting, depth and environment sell a digital product.
